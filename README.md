# Caerfinon-KK-World
Caerfinon's Kerbal Konstructs World

<img width="1250" height="625" alt="Caerfinon-KK-World - Profile Pic" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71fa8755-cd28-4fd3-9acb-972eeead7fcd" />

Airstrips 
 - Maximum launch of 10 ton craft
 - Hangar can store 3 vessels no larger than 25 tons
 - Refuelling facilities at 10 times normal buy costs. Selling not allowed

Single Runway Sites
 - No limits on launch
 - May include helipad launch site restricted to 30 tons
 - Refuelling at normal costs. Selling allowed
 - Barracks available to hire Kerbals for facilities
 - Small scale Science facilities available to invest in
 - Small scale Business facilities available to invest in 

Multiple Runway Sites
 - No limits on launch
 - May include helipad launch site restricted to 30 tons
 - Refuelling at normal costs. Selling allowed
 - Barracks available to hire Kerbals for facilities
 - Large scale Science facilities available to invest in
 - Large scale Business facilities available to invest in

Ports
 - All ports include a Helipad
 - Refuelling at normal costs. Selling allowed
 - No limits on Water Launches
 - Helipad launches restricted to 30 tons
 - Hangar can store 3 vessels no larger than 25 tons
